Privacy Policy – U5 HMS

At U5 HMS (Hospital Management System), we are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of patient and healthcare data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information within our platform.

1. Information We Collect

U5 HMS may collect personal and medical information including patient names, contact details, appointment records, billing information, prescriptions, laboratory results, and healthcare history necessary for hospital management operations.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used strictly for healthcare administration, patient management, appointment scheduling, billing, reporting, communication, and improving healthcare service delivery.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect all data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Access to sensitive information is restricted to authorized personnel only.

4. Confidentiality of Medical Records

Patient medical records are treated with strict confidentiality and are only accessible by authorized healthcare professionals in accordance with applicable healthcare regulations and privacy standards.

5. Third-Party Services

U5 HMS does not sell or share patient information with third parties except where required for healthcare operations, legal obligations, or authorized integrations.

6. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials and ensuring secure access to the system.

7. Updates to This Policy

U5 HMS reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the platform indicates acceptance of any revised terms.

8. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact the U5 HMS support team.
